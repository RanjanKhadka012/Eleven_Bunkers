The sky burned in a single night…
and the world broke under falling fire.

Cities vanished beneath shockwaves.
Forests burned.
The horizon still glows where fragments continue to fall.

Now the surface is unstable.
Ash darkens the air.
Impact zones shift the land… and every tremor could mean another strike is near.

You found a bunker built to survive disaster…
but not everyone can stay.

Only a few can take shelter…
and every choice matters more with each passing hour.

Every person here may help the group endure the collapse…
or make survival even more fragile.

Choose carefully…

Because in this world…
the next impact could decide everything.

The decision starts now.
