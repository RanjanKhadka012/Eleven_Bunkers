Winter never ends…
The cold seeps through concrete, through walls… through you.
Every breath turns to frost in the dim light.

Outside… the world is frozen.
Preserved.
And not everything buried in the ice… should be found again.

A sudden freeze took the planet in minutes.
No warning. No escape.
Only those who reached shelter survived.

But the cold did more than kill.

It preserved things… ancient pathogens… unknown organisms…
Waiting.

Anything you thaw… could save you…
or destroy everything inside this bunker.

You’ve found shelter… but it won’t hold everyone.

Only a few can enter…
and every decision carries risk.

Every person here brings skills…
and dangers you may not see.

Choose carefully…

Because in this world…
survival isn’t just about staying warm…
it’s about knowing what must remain frozen.

The decision starts now.
