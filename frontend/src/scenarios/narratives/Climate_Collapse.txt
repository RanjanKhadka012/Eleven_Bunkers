Opening:
Winter never ends. The cold gnaws through concrete, and every breath fogs the dim lights. Outside, ice preserves things that should have stayed buried.

Scenario Explanation:
Story: A sudden freeze turned Earth into a killing cold within minutes.
Twist: The ice keeps ancient pathogens and unknown organisms intact; thawing them can doom the bunker.

Ending - Win:
You ration heat, time every expedition, and refuse to thaw what you cannot understand. With careful fuel use and sealed labs, the bunker holds until a pale sun hints at change. The cold retreats a little, and so do your fears.

Ending - Loss:
One desperate burn of extra fuel frees something trapped in ice. It spreads before you can react. The heaters sputter out, and the bunker becomes a frozen tomb filled with quiet, new life.
