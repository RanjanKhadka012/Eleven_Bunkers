Opening:
The sky is carved by alien harvest ships. Below, you hide in a bunker never meant for war beyond Earth. Every signal risks drawing their gaze; every survivor may already be altered.

Scenario Explanation:
Story: Aliens conquered Earth and are harvesting humans as resources.
Twist: Some humans are modified and serve as informants inside survivor groups.

Ending - Win:
You scan, test, and mistrust every rescue. An altered informant is exposed before they signal. A stolen beam cutter opens a path, and the bunker stays cold and dark enough to be ignored. A small human enclave slips the harvest cycle.

Ending - Loss:
An informant signals while you sleep. The ceiling glows, the floor shakes, and you are lifted as inventory. The bunker is cataloged and emptied without a shot.
