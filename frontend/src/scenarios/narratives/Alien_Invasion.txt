The sky is carved by alien harvest ships…
Their shadows sweep the ground… searching.

Below, you hide in a bunker never meant for a war beyond Earth.
Every signal risks drawing their gaze…
and every survivor may not be what they seem.

The invasion is complete.
Humanity is no longer fighting… it is being collected.

They don’t just hunt from above.
Some have already been altered… sent back… watching.

Informants… hidden among survivors.

You’ve found shelter… but it won’t hold everyone.

Only a few can enter…
and one wrong choice could expose you all.

Every person here could help you survive…
or lead the enemy straight to you.

Choose carefully…

Because in this world…
trust can get you harvested.

The decision starts now.
