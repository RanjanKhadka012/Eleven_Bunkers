Above, engines and gunfire tear through the dust…
Warlords trade water for loyalty… and bullets for land.

What remains of civilization is scattered… divided… fighting to survive.

Below, you hide in a bunker built on something rare…
a deep, hidden water reserve.

Water is power.
And power is hunted.

Factions are searching…
watching movements… tracking supplies.

If they find you… it’s over.

You’ve found shelter… but it won’t hold everyone.

Only a few can stay…
and every decision shapes what happens next.

Every person here could help you protect what remains…
or expose it.

Choose carefully…

Because in this world…
one mistake doesn’t just cost lives…
it gives everything away.

The decision starts now.
