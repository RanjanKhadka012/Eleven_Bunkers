Air itself has turned against you…
Every breath carries risk.

Outside, a mutating virus spreads through the air…
fast, invisible… relentless.

Inside the bunker, you are safe… for now.
But safety comes at a cost.

The virus adapts.
The more people gather… the faster it changes.

Distance is survival.
Trust… is uncertain.

You’ve found shelter… but it won’t hold everyone.

Only a few can stay…
and every choice affects more than survival.

Every person here could help contain the threat…
or accelerate it.

Choose carefully…

Because in this world…
one mistake doesn’t just spread the infection…
it ends everything.

The decision starts now.
