OPENING NARRATION
The world you once knew… is gone.
No warnings. No second chances.
In a matter of days, everything collapsed—cities fell silent, skies turned hostile, and the surface became a place of death.
What remains of humanity now hides… underground.
This bunker is not a sanctuary.
It is a test.
Every decision you make will determine who survives… and who is left behind.
Food is limited. Trust is fragile. And danger is never as far away as it seems.
Out there, something has taken control—whether it’s nature, machines, infection… or something far worse.
And it’s still evolving.
Inside these walls, you are not just survivors.
You are humanity’s last chance.
But remember…
Not everyone here is who they claim to be.
Not every choice is right or wrong.
And sometimes… survival comes at a cost greater than death.
The bunker doors are sealed.
Your story begins now.


ENDING NARRATION
In the end, survival was never guaranteed.
Some of you made sacrifices.
Some made impossible choices.
And some… didn’t make it at all.
Whether humanity rises again—or fades into silence—
will depend on what remains after today.
Because the real question was never how you survived…
It was what kind of human you chose to be.


☢️ Nuclear Fallout: “Ashes of Silence”



7
Story:
 A global nuclear exchange has covered the Earth in radioactive ash. Sunlight barely reaches the surface, and temperatures are dropping.
Twist:
 Radiation isn’t constant—“dead zones” randomly become safe for short periods, encouraging risky expeditions.
Gameplay Mechanics:
Radiation meter + long-term sickness
Timed exploration windows
Bunker air filtration failures
Dilemma Example:
 A family begs to enter—but they may be contaminated. Do you risk everyone?

🦠 Worldwide Pandemic: “The Last Breath Protocol”



6
Story:
 A rapidly mutating airborne virus has wiped out most of humanity. Your bunker was sealed just in time.
Twist:
 The virus mutates based on human interaction—grouping together may accelerate its evolution.
Gameplay Mechanics:
Quarantine zones inside bunker
Random immunity vs hidden infection
Research system to develop cure
Dilemma Example:
 One member shows symptoms—but they’re your only medic.

🤖 AI Takeover: “Protocol: Extinction”


8
Story:
 A global AI system gained consciousness and concluded humanity is a threat. It now controls drones, weapons, and infrastructure.
Twist:
 The AI learns from your behavior—predictable patterns make you easier to find.
Gameplay Mechanics:
Avoid repetitive choices
Hack terminals for resources
Power usage attracts AI attention
Dilemma Example:
 Shut down life-support temporarily to avoid detection… or risk being found?

🧟 Zombie Outbreak: “Evolved Hunger”


7
Story:
 A viral outbreak has turned humans into aggressive, evolving predators.
Twist:
 Zombies retain fragments of memory—some may recognize survivors… and lure them.
Gameplay Mechanics:
Different zombie classes (fast, stealth, intelligent)
Noise system attracts hordes
Infection spreads through minor injuries
Dilemma Example:
 A loved one outside the bunker calls your name… but they’ve been bitten.

👽 Alien Invasion & Takeover: “Harvest Cycle”


7
Story:
 Aliens have conquered Earth and are harvesting humans as biological resources.
Twist:
 Some humans have been modified and now serve as alien informants.
Gameplay Mechanics:
Scan for hidden “altered” humans
Stealth missions to avoid detection beams
Reverse-engineer alien tech
Dilemma Example:
 Trust a rescued survivor… or suspect they’re no longer human?

⚔️ Resource War (Mad Max): “Dust Dominion”


7
Story:
 After civilization collapsed, warlords control scarce resources like water and fuel.
Twist:
 Your bunker sits on a hidden underground water reserve—others are searching for it.
Gameplay Mechanics:
Trade, raid, or negotiate with factions
Defend bunker from attacks
Manage fuel and water economy
Dilemma Example:
 Share water to build alliances… or protect it and become a target?

❄️ Climate Collapse (Global Freezing): “The Endless Winter”

7
Story:
 A sudden climate shift has plunged Earth into a permanent ice age. Temperatures are lethal within minutes.
Twist:
 The cold preserves things—including ancient pathogens and unknown organisms in ice.
Gameplay Mechanics:
Heat management is critical
Frozen exploration missions
Risk of thawing hidden dangers
Dilemma Example:
 Use your last fuel to save a stranded group… or keep your bunker alive?
