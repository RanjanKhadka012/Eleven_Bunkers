Opening:
The world claws at the bunker walls. The infected still remember enough to knock, to call, to lure. Hunger has a voice, and it knows your names.

Scenario Explanation:
Story: A viral outbreak twisted people into fast, evolving predators.
Twist: Some infected retain shards of memory and use them to bait survivors.

Ending - Win:
You move silent, manage noise, and burn every infected wound. When the last horde wanders off in confusion, you emerge with blades dull and hearts hammering. The dead are no longer outside your door.

Ending - Loss:
A whisper you recognize pulls someone to the hatch. One slip, one bite, and infection races through cramped corridors. The bunker becomes another nest of hunger.
