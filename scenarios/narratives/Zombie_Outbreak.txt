The world claws at the bunker walls…
Something is out there… and it remembers you.

The infected are not mindless.
They listen… they mimic… they call out in familiar voices.

Hunger has learned to speak.

A viral outbreak has turned the surface into a hunting ground.
Those who remain have changed… faster, stronger… unpredictable.

And some still remember just enough…
to lure the living.

You’ve found shelter… but it won’t hold everyone.

Only a few can stay…
and every choice could open the door.

Every person here could help you survive…
or bring the threat inside.

Choose carefully…

Because in this world…
what sounds human…
may no longer be.

The decision starts now.
