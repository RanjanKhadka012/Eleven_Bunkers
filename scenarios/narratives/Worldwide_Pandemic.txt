Opening:
Air itself has turned against you. Outside, a mutating virus hunts through every breath. Inside, trust cracks as coughs echo in metal halls.

Scenario Explanation:
Story: A fast-evolving airborne pathogen wiped out the surface. Your bunker sealed just in time.
Twist: The virus changes faster when people cluster. Quarantine and distance are as critical as food.

Ending - Win:
You isolate early, rotate duties, and a tiny research breakthrough slows the mutation. When supplies run low, immunity markers confirm a handful can rebuild. The bunker opens to a world ready to heal.

Ending - Loss:
One breach, one handshake too many. The pathogen blooms inside the bunker. Doors seal, alarms fail, and the vents carry the end to every room.
