Opening:
The world you once knew is gone. A global nuclear exchange has buried the sky in ash, and the bunker air tastes like metal. Every choice you make is a test of who gets to breathe another day.

Scenario Explanation:
Story: Radioactive ash blocks the sun and temperatures keep falling. Dead zones sometimes calm just long enough for desperate expeditions.
Twist: Radiation pockets shift. Safe corridors can vanish without warning, making every outing a gamble.

Ending - Win:
You time the dead zones, purge the filters, and ration the air. The bunker holds, the ash thins, and the surviving few step out into a muted dawn. Humanity has a fragile foothold.

Ending - Loss:
The filters clog, a bad reading opens the blast door at the wrong moment, and the bunker fills with poison. The last sounds are Geiger clicks fading into silence.
