The world you once knew is gone…
A global nuclear exchange has buried the sky in ash.
The air tastes like metal… and every breath is measured.

Sunlight barely reaches the ground.
Temperatures keep falling.
Survival depends on what little remains… inside the bunker.

But the outside is not entirely still.

Radiation shifts.
Dead zones appear… and disappear without warning.
A path that was safe moments ago… can become lethal.

Every expedition is a gamble.

You’ve found shelter… but it won’t hold everyone.

Only a few can enter…
and every choice determines who gets to breathe another day.

Every person here brings something valuable…
but also risk.

Choose carefully…

Because in this world…
one mistake doesn’t just cost a life…
it can cost them all.

The decision starts now.
